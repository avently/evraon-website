# Account Deletion Policy for Evraon

If you would like to delete your account and all associated personal data, you can request deletion by following the steps below.

## Data Deletion Process:
1. **Send an Email Request:** Click [Request Account Deletion](mailto:contact@evraon.com?subject=Delete%20account) (or send an email manually to **contact@evraon.com** with the subject line **"Delete account"**).
2. **Provide Your Details:** In the body of the email, please specify the exact email address used to register your account in the app.
3. **Identity Verification:** To ensure security and protect your privacy, we will send a confirmation message to your registered email address. This prevents unauthorized individuals from requesting the deletion of your account.
4. **Finalization:** Once you reply to our confirmation email to verify your identity, the deletion process will begin. 

## Timeline and Data Handling:
* **Timeline:** The account and all linked data will be permanently deleted within **30 days** from the moment we receive your final email confirmation.
* **What data will be deleted:** Your user profile, login credentials, and all historical usage data collected within the app.
* **What data will be retained:** We do not retain any personal data after the deletion is complete, except for records required to comply with statutory legal or financial obligations.
